## Global Warming: Causes, Consequences, and International Response
### Definition and Causes
Global warming refers to the long-term increase in Earth's average surface temperature, primarily caused by human activities such as burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial operations, which lead to greenhouse gas emissions.
### Key Contributors
The main causes of global warming include greenhouse gas emissions, energy production from coal and fossil fuels, and synthetic nitrogen fertilizers.
### Consequences and Environmental Impacts
The consequences of global warming include rising sea levels, threatening coastal communities, and extreme weather events.
### Importance of Natural Carbon Sinks
Preserving natural carbon sinks is crucial in mitigating the effects of global warming.
### International Response
The Paris Agreement aims to limit global temperature increase to well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels, with efforts to limit the increase to 1.5°C, highlighting the need for international cooperation to address the issue.
